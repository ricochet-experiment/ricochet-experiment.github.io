### The Laue-Langevin Institute

The Ricochet experiment is located at the [Laue-Langevin Institute](https://www.ill.eu/for-all-users/instruments/instruments-list/ricochet/description/instrument-layout), holding the only nuclear reactor in France entirely dedicated to scientific research.
The setup is installed at 8.8 m from the nuclear core, subject to a high flux of electron antineutrinos created during the fission process. 
The reactor is active during 3 cycles per year, summing to an average of six months, providing ideal conditions to characterize both the CEvNS signal and the environmental background.
